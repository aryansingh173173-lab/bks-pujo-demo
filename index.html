The stack underneath

A Pujo that runs on software we wrote

Most festivals are coordinated on WhatsApp groups and paper. This one is coordinated on OmniDEL.ai, a work platform KarmYog built and owns, with an AI assistant called MahAcharyaJi sitting inside it. The team calls it the Acharya. It speaks Bangla, Hindi and English, and it works by voice, because the people using it are standing in a field or on a construction site, not sitting at a desk.

A

Building the Pujo

Site planning, vendor coordination, budgets, permissions, volunteer rosters and daily field reports all live in one place. Every karyakarta sees the tasks that belong to them, marks them off from the field, and the whole picture updates for everyone at once.

Nothing about this Pujo is being run out of a spreadsheet that one person owns. That matters to a sponsor for a plain reason: what gets promised in a meeting is traceable to a task with a name against it.

B

Working with farmers

The same platform carries the farmer programme. Each integrated farm has a record. The crop, pond, livestock and market cycles turn into scheduled tasks and reminders, delivered in the farmer’s own language, by voice where reading is a barrier.

Farmers ask the Acharya practical questions — what to do about a pond that has turned, when to move a planting, how to price at the market this week — and get an answer in Bangla or Hindi immediately, at the hour they are actually working, which is rarely the hour an extension officer is available.

C

Learning and productivity

Integrated Farming only works if the farmer learns a system, not a single crop. The Acharya carries that teaching in small pieces across the year, tied to what the farmer is doing that week, rather than as a training day that is forgotten by the next season.

Productivity follows from the same loop. Records make yields and costs visible. Visible numbers make the next decision better. This is how a ₹1 lakh seed becomes a farm that keeps standing after the money is spent.

When you sponsor this Pujo you are not funding a one-week event with a technology slide attached. You are backing a project where the software, the farm and the festival were designed together, by the same people, and where the system is already in daily use.

Why is farming in a Puja?
Because the Puja is when Bengal gathers. This gathering names the farmer, recognises the farmers nobody photographs, and then shows Integrated Farming so that livelihood is something visitors walk through rather than read on a board.
What is Integrated Farming?
Crop, animals, water and market held together on one holding, so the waste from one part becomes the input for the next. On a small West Bengal holding that is the difference between one income and several.
What is OmniDEL.ai?
The work platform this whole project runs on. KarmYog for the 21st Century built it and owns it. Tasks, teams, sites, vendors, farm records and field reporting all sit in it, and an AI assistant called MahAcharyaJi sits inside it. It is at omnidel.ai and it is in daily use.
What does the AI actually do?
Two jobs. For the team it turns plans into tasks, keeps the field and the office looking at the same picture, and takes reports from people who are standing outdoors. For farmers it answers practical questions in their own language, carries the crop, pond and livestock calendar as reminders, and keeps a record the farmer can use to make the next decision.
Which languages does it work in?
Bangla, Hindi and English, spoken or typed. Voice matters here: many of the people using it are working with their hands, and some read less comfortably than they speak.
Is the AI a gimmick for the festival?
No. It is how the Pujo is being built. Remove it and the project does not run. It is also the reason the platform does not end in October — the same records and task cycles carry the village farm programme through the year.
Why is sponsorship relevant to my organisation?
Because it puts your organisation inside a public conversation about farmer recognition and rural livelihood during the one week the whole city is out on the street. The value is qualitative. We do not sell reach.

No. The public week is in October, and the money seeds village farms that outlast it. Visibility during the week is part of the arrangement, not the whole of it.
