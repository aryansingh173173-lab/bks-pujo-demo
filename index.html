Why this is different

Most festival sponsorship is inventory: a few nights, a hoarding, a logo on a pandal. This briefing is not that rate card.

Bharatiya Krishak Samaj Pujo places the farmer and Integrated Farming inside Durga Puja, when Bengal is outdoors together. The intended setting is decision-makers and the public in one living week. That setting is intended. It is not a guaranteed attendance, media, or return figure.

Questions a board will ask

What is Bharatiya Krishak Samaj Pujo?

A seasonal Durga Puja that puts the farmer in the gathering, shows Integrated Farming as livelihood, and invites neighbours, supporters and partners to take part.

Who is organising it, and what is Bharatiya Krishak Samaj’s role?

Organised by KarmYog for the 21st Century, with Bharatiya Krishak Samaj as organising partner. Bharatiya Krishak Samaj is not the title sponsor.

Who is KarmYog for the 21st Century?

The organiser of this Pujo. KarmYog for the 21st Century Foundation also organised Durga Puja Mahotsav 2025 at the IIT Kharagpur Research Park — historical / reference context for craft and gathering. Those photographs are not the 2026 Bharatiya Krishak Samaj pandal unless clearly captioned as such.

Why is the farming story inside a Puja?

Because the Puja is when Bengal gathers. This gathering names the farmer, recognises unnamed farmers, then shows Integrated Farming so livelihood is not left as decoration.

Why should my organisation care?

Because the week can place your organisation inside a public conversation about farmer visibility and Integrated Farming — not only beside a board. Relevance is qualitative. Reach and ROI are not guaranteed.

Is this conventional four-day advertising?

The civic window is four days. The positioning is a seasonal platform meant to continue. Later-year programmes are not published here.

What is the longer-term proposition?

A continuing conversation around Puja, farmer, Integrated Farming and mobilisation. Multi-year programme detail is not published here.

Are sponsorship packages confirmed?

Working class figures (for example Title ≈ ₹10 lakh, Category ≈ ₹2.5 lakh) are provisional conversation aids. Treat them as discussion points, not a locked rate card.

What is confirmed, and what is only proposed?

Published here: event name, organiser, organising partner, civic dates, Kolkata, title status pending confirmation, and that payment is not taken through this experience. Proposed: ₹1 lakh seed, in full or ₹20,000 every two months — not collected here. Target: about 5,000 farms or patrons. ₹50 crore is the arithmetic of that target, not funds already raised. Provisional: Title ₹10 lakh and Category ₹2.5 lakh.

What happens after I express interest?

An interest note downloads to your device. You can email it to contact@bkswbengal.org if you wish to continue. That begins a conversation — it is not a booking or payment.

Is payment available through this website?

No. Payment is not taken through this experience. There is no checkout and no booking.

What is FarmTech and AgriTech here?

Careful tools inside the Integrated Farming model: monitoring, drip, pond energy and diagnostics where they help a small farm. Model-based sketch. No vendor list. No guaranteed yields.

Is government endorsement claimed?

No. Institutional engagement is a separate briefing door. This sponsor experience does not claim government partnership, endorsement or scheme.

Where and when is it?

Kolkata, West Bengal. Civic window 16–20 October 2026. Exact Puja venue will be announced through official channels. The Munshir Bheri address is the demonstration / farm location — not automatically the confirmed pandal.
